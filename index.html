YooMoney

Senior Go Engineer · fintech, payment platform · Jan 2024 – Present
payment core
  • Designed a saga-based payout flow without 2PC, backed by strict idempotency keys — eliminated double-spend risk from retries and partial failures, delivering exactly-once settlement on money movement.
  • Built a transactional outbox → Kafka → ClickHouse pipeline that moved analytical and event traffic off the production ledger database, cutting PostgreSQL load by ~30%.
