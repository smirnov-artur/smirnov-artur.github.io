弐Under the pagoda

Tea in the hillside pavilion

Forty-five minutes with our tea master in a four-and-a-half mat room. Made for first-timers: we show you where to sit and what to do with the bowl.

  1. The garden path

    Stepping stones up from the bridge, past the torii.

  2. The stone basin

    Rinse your hands, leave the day outside.

  3. Sweet and thick tea

    A seasonal wagashi, then koicha from one shared bowl.

  4. Thin tea

    A bowl each, and time to ask about the utensils.
