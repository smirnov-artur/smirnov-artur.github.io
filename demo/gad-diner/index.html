How it's made

the long wayThree minutes
on the spindle

A proper shake is thick enough to hold the straw and thin enough to drink. That takes real ice cream and a little patience.

01

Churned in the morning

Ice cream is made before we open: cream, milk, sugar, eggs. It's the base for every shake on the board.

02

Spun, not blended

A spindle mixer turns the shake for three minutes in a steel cup, so it stays thick and never goes icy.

03

Served with the tin

What doesn't fit in the glass comes to the table in the steel cup. That part's a free refill.

“The first sip is the part worth stopping time for.”

That's the whole idea of the place: sit down, slow down, finish your shake.
