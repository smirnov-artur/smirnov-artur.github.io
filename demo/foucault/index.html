01 · the arithmetic

Two numbers and a sine.

The Earth turns a full circle against the stars in 23 h 56 min 04 s. That is 15.041 degrees an hour — not 15. A pendulum standing at the pole sees all of it. A pendulum standing on the equator sees none of it, because there the floor does not rotate about the vertical at all.

Everything in between is the sine of the latitude. Nothing else enters: not the mass of the bob, not the length of the cable, not the size of the push. The cable length sets only the period, and the period sets only how often a peg gets a chance to fall.

02 · the pegs

The pegs are the proof.

Seventy-two pegs stand in a ring, one every five degrees. The bob knocks down whatever happens to stand in its plane. The plane never moves. The ring moves under it, carried by the building, carried by the Earth.

So the gap that opens in the ring is not an effect. It is a measurement: the angle the floor has travelled since the push, written down by the instrument itself, in five-degree steps.

Push the bob to start the record.

Amplitude decays slowly — a time constant of 1500 seconds. Once the swing falls below 80 % of the push it no longer reaches the ring, and the record stops. That is why a real hall has an attendant.
