What it connects to

Where it learns your products

  • An xlsx or csv file with any columns.
  • A Google Sheets link: it re-reads every 10 minutes, so stock stays fresh with no exports.
  • A shop-info text and your own rules: "offer underlay with laminate flooring", "call me in for wholesale".
  • Your past replies in Avito chats as a tone sample — it writes just as briefly as you do.

Where leads go

  • To you on Telegram or to your team's group — copies of the replies show up there too, and any team member can step in.
  • Bitrix24: a lead via an incoming webhook.
  • amoCRM: a deal with a contact and a note.
  • A webhook to any service, including Google Sheets via Apps Script.
  • Export all leads to Excel with one command.

You can connect several Avito accounts, handy for Avito specialists: each has its own chats, rules and leads. For specialists and agencies, the bot has a referral link via the /partner command. There's no chat broadcasting, and there never will be — it breaks Avito's rules.

How this is different from what already exists

Avito's own Assistant

Free, built into the chats. Answers common questions from the listing text and stays quiet while you're online. Doesn't know about stock, sets, or delivery to another city unless it's written in the listing.

Bot builders

₽500 to ₽2,500 a month. You build the scripts and knowledge base yourself, and many charge separately per AI reply. Without a link to your spreadsheet, the bot doesn't know what's in stock today.

This responder

Code — not the AI — pulls the size, season and brand from the message, and the same code finds the rows in your spreadsheet. The AI only assembles the reply from what it found, so it can't make up a price or a stock number. I set it up, you edit the spreadsheet.

On the Assistant: Avito's help article. Builder prices: public price lists of avChat, AvBot, aChatBot as of September 2026.

What it does and doesn't do

Does

  • Understands "205/55 r16", "205 55 16", and "non-studded ones?" after the previous message.
  • Quotes the price per tire and per set, the stock count, cheaper and better alternatives.
  • Answers on address, hours, delivery, payment and returns from your reference text.
  • Discounts, wholesale, returns and complaints go to you on Telegram; the buyer is told it'll check and get back.
  • Sends you a lead once the buyer is ready to buy.

Doesn't

  • Doesn't mention what's not in the spreadsheet, and won't promise "on order" if that's not how you work.
  • Doesn't give out a phone number or invite people to other messengers — moving the conversation off Avito breaks the platform's rules. No links either.
  • Doesn't haggle on your behalf.
  • Doesn't lie about being human if asked directly.
  • Doesn't reply in a chat where you're already writing yourself.
