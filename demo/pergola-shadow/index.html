The arithmetic, in full

Standard solar geometry — Cooper's equation for declination, as given in Duffie & Beckman, Solar Engineering of Thermal Processes. Nothing is fitted and nothing is tuned: every number below comes out of these four lines.

δ  = 23.45° · sin( 360/365 · (284 + n) )          declination, n = day of year
H  = 15° · (t − 12)                                hour angle, t = solar hour
α  = asin( sinφ·sinδ + cosφ·cosδ·cosH )              sun altitude, φ = latitude
A  = atan2( sinH , cosH·sinφ − tanδ·cosφ )           azimuth, from south
L  = h / tan α                                     shadow length, h = height

The table sits 1.9 m south of the posts, 0.75 m tall. It is in shade while the shadow reaches past it — that is the whole test, and it is the question a customer actually asks before buying a pergola.
