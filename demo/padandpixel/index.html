01 · Mock reviews

Mock reviews and game evaluations from the critics publishers watch.

Under NDA, we tell you what the press and your players will say while there’s still time to act on it.

Request the rate card
  1. 01

    Signature Mock Review

    A full pre-release review written personally by Brian Crecente: played to completion, scored, and argued. Predicted Metacritic band and Steam sentiment outlook, strengths and weaknesses, prioritized fixes, comparables analysis, and a live debrief with your team.

    Fixed fee by game tier
  2. 02

    Critical Panel

    Three to five critics drawn from our 50+ reviewer bench independently write mock reviews of your game, each selected for genre expertise, region, and generation, topped with a Crecente-written synthesis: where the critics converge, where they split, and what to fix, lead with, and brace for.

    Three to five critics
  3. 03

    Game Evaluation

    For titles still in development, vertical slice through beta: a deep analytical report on vision coherence, systems, genre positioning, and audience, delivered while every finding is still actionable. No score predictions before beta; we don’t sell false precision.

    Vertical slice to beta
  4. 04

    Readiness Review

    A fast, focused read on a single beat before the public sees it: a Steam Next Fest demo, store page, trailer, or announcement package. Written by a vetted senior critic from our bench and edited by Crecente, delivered in one week.

    From $1,500 · one week
  5. 05

    Staged Evaluation Program

    Critical checkpoints from vertical slice through gold, closing with a full mock review or panel at near-gold, so launch reviews are a confirmation, not a surprise. Retainer clients get priority scheduling.

    Vertical slice to gold
