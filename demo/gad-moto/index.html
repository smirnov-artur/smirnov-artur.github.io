How a trip works

You bring the riding. Permits, bikes, fuel, beds and the truck are ours to sort.

Visa invitation letter, local permits, insurance for the bike, and a fitting call so the bike is set to your height.

3

Ride

Land in Islamabad or Gilgit. From day one the bike, fuel, lodges, breakfast and dinner, and the support truck are covered.

The bike

We ride small singles because the pass takes away what big engines are good at.

At 4,693 m an engine loses almost half its power.

A big bike turns into a heavy bike. A light single keeps pulling in first and second, and when it tips over on gravel you lift it yourself.

What's in the price

One price per rider, twin share. Single rooms where the lodge has them.

Included

  • Bike, fuel and oil for the whole route
  • Lead rider and a mechanic
  • Support 4×4 with your bags and the spares
  • Lodges and guesthouses, breakfast and dinner
  • Permits and park fees
  • Satellite messenger, pulse oximeter and oxygen in the truck

Not included

  • Flights to Islamabad or Gilgit
  • Visa fee
  • Riding gear (rental set: $80 for the trip)
  • Lunch on the road
  • Travel insurance
